Research interests and background

My academic focus is deep learning applied to perception and prediction: how models learn useful structure from images and tabular signals, and how we can tell whether they have learned it reliably.

Beneath the applied work sits a deliberate foundation in algorithms, data structures and the mathematics behind machine learning: linear algebra, probability and optimization. I want to understand why a method works, not only that it does.
